# Training and Certification Changelog

## [1.1.0.0] - 2026-04-07

### Added
- **20 entities** with **430+ fields** supporting comprehensive learning, credentialing, and qualification management:
  - Learning delivery entities (Training Course, Training Course Requirement, Training Objective, Training Instructor, Academic Term, Training Session, Training Enrollment, Training Session Attendance, Training Completion) for course catalog, scheduling, and attendance
  - Learning paths (Learning Path, Learning Path Course) for curated course sequences and skill development
  - Certification entities (Training Certificate, Training Certificate Requirement, Training Certificate Achievement, Training Certificate Renewal) for credential lifecycle management
  - Academic programs (Academic Program, Academic Program Requirement, Academic Program Completion) for degree and diploma programs
  - Qualification requirements (Qualification Requirement, Qualification Requirement Item) for role-based and operational eligibility enforcement
- **25 choice sets** for course types, attendance statuses, academic honors, delivery methods, certificate types, and program levels
- Support for prerequisites and corequisites, attendance tracking, grade and scoring, certificate expiration and renewal, continuing education requirements, and flexible qualification enforcement

### Changed
- Initial baseline release supporting higher education, workforce development, compliance training, and professional certification programs
